Detailed Description

Proxy class controlling access to plant inventory operations.

InventoryProxy provides controlled access to PlantInventory functionality, acting as a protection proxy that can enforce business rules and access control. It coordinates with SalesFloorObserver to maintain consistency between inventory and sales floor displays.
