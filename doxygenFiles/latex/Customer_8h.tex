\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Represents a \mbox{\hyperlink{classCustomer}{Customer}} who can place orders and make staff queries. 

The \mbox{\hyperlink{classCustomer}{Customer}} class models a retail customer with a name and a list of previous orders. Customers can interact with staff by submitting queries and can review their order history.
