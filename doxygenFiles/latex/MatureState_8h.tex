Defines the \mbox{\hyperlink{classMatureState}{Mature\+State}} class representing a plant’s healthy phase. 

In this state, the plant thrives under regular care and sustenance. If its resources fall below minimum thresholds, it transitions to \mbox{\hyperlink{classDistressedState}{Distressed\+State}}.
