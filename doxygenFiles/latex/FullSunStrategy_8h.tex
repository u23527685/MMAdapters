\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Concrete sunlight strategy applying full sunlight exposure to a plant. 

Implements the \mbox{\hyperlink{classSunlightStrategy}{Sunlight\+Strategy}} interface to provide maximum sunlight exposure to a plant. Increases sunlight level by 60\% per day, capped at the plant\textquotesingle{}s maximum allowed sunlight.

\begin{DoxyNote}{Note}
Helps simulate sun-\/loving plants that thrive in full light.
\end{DoxyNote}
\begin{DoxyWarning}{Warning}
Exceeding the maximum sunlight value resets it to the limit.
\end{DoxyWarning}
