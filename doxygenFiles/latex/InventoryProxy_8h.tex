\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Proxy class controlling access to plant inventory operations. 

\mbox{\hyperlink{classInventoryProxy}{Inventory\+Proxy}} provides controlled access to \mbox{\hyperlink{classPlantInventory}{Plant\+Inventory}} functionality, acting as a protection proxy that can enforce business rules and access control. It coordinates with \mbox{\hyperlink{classSalesFloorObserver}{Sales\+Floor\+Observer}} to maintain consistency between inventory and sales floor displays.
