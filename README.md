<h2 style="color:#2E7D32;">🌿 Project Description</h2>

<p style="color:#1B5E20; text-align:justify; line-height:1.6;">
<b>Plantopia</b> is a gamified <b>Plant Nursery Simulator</b> inspired by the vibrant renewal of spring in South Africa.  
In Plantopia, growth isn’t just something that happens in the soil — it’s a living, dynamic system that blossoms through thoughtful design, teamwork, and creativity.
</p>

<p style="color:#1B5E20; text-align:justify; line-height:1.6;">
The simulator models a thriving nursery ecosystem where <b>plants</b>, <b>staff</b>, and <b>customers</b> interact seamlessly.  
Using <b>C++</b> and <b>object-oriented design patterns</b>, Plantopia transforms real-world complexity into a flexible, maintainable, and scalable digital experience.
</p>

<ul style="color:#1B5E20; line-height:1.6; list-style-type:square;">
  <li><b>Greenhouse Management:</b> Model diverse plant species, care routines, and seasonal growth cycles.</li>
  <li><b>Staff Coordination:</b> Manage inventory, plant care, and assist customers efficiently.</li>
  <li><b>Customer Experience:</b> Personalize plant selections and handle transactions dynamically.</li>
</ul>

<p style="color:#1B5E20; text-align:justify;">
Through gamification, players “level up” by optimizing workflows, nurturing plants, and satisfying customers — cultivating not only a digital nursery but also strong design thinking and collaboration.
</p>
</div>
