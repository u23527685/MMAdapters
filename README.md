<h2 style="color:#1B5E20;">🌿 Project Description</h2>

<p style="color:#33691E;">
Welcome to <b>Plantopia</b>, a gamified <b>Plant Nursery Simulator</b> inspired by the vibrant renewal of spring in South Africa.  
In Plantopia, growth isn’t just something that happens in the soil — it’s a living, dynamic system that blossoms through thoughtful design, teamwork, and creativity.
</p>

<p style="color:#33691E;">
Plantopia models the intricate world of a modern nursery, where <b>plants</b>, <b>staff</b>, and <b>customers</b> form a thriving ecosystem.
Each element plays a vital role:
</p>

<ul style="color:#1B5E20;">
<li><b>Greenhouse Management:</b> Nurture plants through unique care strategies, growth cycles, and seasonal behaviors.</li>
<li><b>Staff Coordination:</b> Employees monitor plant health, track inventory, and assist customers through adaptive roles.</li>
<li><b>Customer Experience:</b> Visitors explore, personalize their purchases, and interact dynamically with staff and stock availability.</li>
</ul>

<p style="color:#33691E;">
Built using <b>C++</b> and grounded in <b>object-oriented design</b> and <b>software patterns</b>, Plantopia transforms complex nursery operations into a flexible, maintainable, and scalable system.  
As the nursery grows, so do your opportunities — unlock new plant types, expand your greenhouse, and cultivate deeper customer experiences.
</p>
</div>
